A little about us

Made by hand.
Made with heart.

The Little Atelier is a small handmade brand built around thoughtful details, soft textures and pieces that feel personal. From crochet hair accessories to little gifts and home accents, every creation celebrates the beauty of making something by hand.

Rooted in Bandel, West Bengal, our aim is simple: to make everyday things feel a little more special.

A few quick answers

Good to know

Need more detail? Send us a message and we will help you with your order.

Are the products handmade?

Yes. The products featured here are handmade using yarn, wool and other craft materials. Small variations are part of the handmade process.

Can I request a different colour?

Custom colour requests can be discussed directly with us. Availability and any additional time required will be confirmed before the order is accepted.

How long will my order take?

Making and delivery timelines depend on the product and destination. We will confirm the expected timeline with you before you place the order.

How do I pay?

Payment instructions will be shared directly after we confirm the product, quantity and delivery details. Do not make a payment until the order details are confirmed.

Can I place a gift or bulk order?

Yes, send us the product details, quantity and date you need the order by. We will let you know what is possible.
